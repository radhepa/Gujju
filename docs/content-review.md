# Content review

The sentence collections, the stories and the word list were written in one pass and then read through again, sentence by sentence, for spelling, grammar, naturalness, translation, the cloze answers and transliteration. Everything also has to pass the [100 accuracy checks](accuracy.md).

What's here: 462 practice sentences (everyday 163, out and about 167, conversations 132), four stories (69 sentences) and about 1,800 glossed words, plus the course units 7 to 10.

## What the read-through changed

- **Cloze answers that could also be right**, the most important kind of fix: ev-150, oa-009, oa-010, oa-153, km-15, cv-087 and cv-095 each had a "wrong" answer that made a valid sentence. All are replaced.
- **Spelling**: નવરાત્રિ (short િ, from Sanskrit રાત્રિ) everywhere; સાઇકલ; loanwords with ૅ/ૉ in the dictionary style (મૅચ, બૅટરી, ગૅસ, સૉરી, ડૉક્ટર).
- **Naturalness**: a story opening that needed એક (tc-01); કેસર પૈસા ગણે છે (km-11); અમે added in cv-024; દીવાઓથી for "with lamps" (cv-022).
- **Translations**: "Who is in your family?" instead of "Who all…"; the cousin in cv-031 made clear; a backwards grammar note in oa-146.
- **Course notes**: "for a boy" / "for things" are now masculine / neuter, because મોટો goes with any masculine word and ચા is feminine. Notes added on જય શ્રી કૃષ્ણ (also goodbye), માફ કરજો (also excuse me), ચાલો and બહેન (said "ben").

## Rules now applied across the app

- ફ is **ph**, like kh, gh and bh, even though many words say it like f (the letter's own note says so).
- ઈ after another vowel is **i**: bhaai, rasoi, gai, Mumbai. The vowel sign ી is **ee**, and ીએ is **iye** (chhiye, mammiye).
- The silent vowel inside a word is dropped the way it's said: ghar, chhokro, deekro, aapNe, saambhLe, paaghDee, aangLeeo.
